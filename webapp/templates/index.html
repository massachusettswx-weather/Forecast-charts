About the data

Forecast models

ECMWF open data (AIFS ENS 51 members, AIFS Single, IFS control, EPS 50 perturbed members) and NOAA open data (GFS deterministic, GEFS mean + control + 30 perturbed members). Raw GRIB2 is fetched straight from the archives; only the u @ 10 hPa message is downloaded, using the published index files.

Climatology

ERA5 1991–2020 reanalysis, zonal-mean u at 60˚N, daily 12 Z, with ±1σ and ±2σ across the 30 years. Cached locally, so every level and latitude is an instant lookup.

How it updates

The watcher polls every 15 minutes and publishes a cycle as soon as every model for it has landed; a deadline sweep at 03, 09, 15 and 21 UTC (9 h after each initialisation) guarantees a rebuild even if a poll was delayed. It resolves the newest published cycle, fetches only what is missing, and re-renders these charts.

06z and 18z cycles

ECMWF publishes the IFS control and the EPS only out to 144 h in the 06z and 18z runs, so those two models are left off the charts for those cycles. AIFS, GFS and GEFS run the full 15–16 days and are shown in full.
